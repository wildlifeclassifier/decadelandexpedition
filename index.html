What this is

A static build of the TDE 2.0 survey-coverage dashboard: 12 ecoregion survey grids over Saudi Arabia, the wildlife records that fall into each cell, and how far they go towards the species an expert range says should be there. Everything recomputes in the browser from the record level; there is no server.

GPS collar telemetry is display-only: fixes never count as records, species or sites. Only the higher-quality collars are kept (PDOP ≤ 8, deployment and dead-tail trims, the six longest collars per species and cluster with ≥ 100 live fixes, plus the single qualifying Nubian ibex collar).

Definitions

  • Survey cell: grid status Random or Systematic. Other cells are shown pale and never count towards coverage.
  • With data: at least one record of any source falls in the cell. Field-surveyed: at least one field-tier record.
  • Species: distinct names at rank species or subspecies. Genus-only, unidentified and unparsed names stay in the record counts but never in species counts.
  • Expected species: a species whose expert range contains the cell centroid (the strict rule). A touch-any variant is in the data pack.
  • Records in the headline, cell and ecoregion views count record-to-cell links: a record that falls where two ecoregion grids overlap is counted in both cells, exactly as in the Shiny app.
  • Accumulation curves: by survey cell, the mean (± 1 sd) over 50 random cell orderings; by record, individual-based rarefaction (Hurlbert).
  • Counts of individuals are carried in the data but never displayed.

Data files

The site reads the compact JSON and GeoJSON files in data/. The hand-off pack that ships with the deploy bundle (download/) carries the same content in full resolution and open formats: a GeoPackage with grid cells, ecoregions, protected areas, wetland registry, species ranges, telemetry segments and observation points; the same layers as GeoJSON; and every table as CSV, including the unified observations.csv with the source-specific attributes as JSON.
